# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

**Expected behavior:**

- Be respectful and considerate
- Provide constructive feedback
- Assume good faith

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Deliberate misinformation or malicious contributions

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. They may take action they deem appropriate, including warning or banning contributors who violate these standards.

## No real-time moderation

This project does not guarantee real-time moderation. Reports will be reviewed as soon as practicable on a best-effort basis.

## Reporting

Do not include sensitive personal details in public issues or discussions.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, use a private reporting channel when one is available. If no private channel is currently listed, open a minimal public issue asking maintainers to provide a private reporting channel, without naming people involved, sharing personal information, or including sensitive details.

All reports will be handled with discretion on a best-effort basis.
